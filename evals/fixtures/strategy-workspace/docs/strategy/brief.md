# Strategy brief: local research notes

Status: accepted 2026-10.

## Target

Two-person research teams who keep sensitive notes on their own machines.

## Position

Local-first capture with explicit export, sold as a one-time licence rather than a hosted
subscription. The differentiator is ownership of the data, not feature breadth.

## Evidence and limits

- Five interviews with research teams; two currently pay for a hosted tool.
- No pricing test has been run.
