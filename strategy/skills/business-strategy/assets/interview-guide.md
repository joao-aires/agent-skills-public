# Interview Guide

A reusable guide, not a script to read aloud. Adapt wording to the participant; keep the order. Method: [customer discovery](../references/customer-discovery.md).

## Opening

- Purpose: understand how the work happens today, not to sell anything.
- No right answers; specifics are more useful than opinions.
- Permission to record and to quote without attribution.
- Time available.

## Context

1. What is your role, and what work do you own?
2. Who else is involved when this comes up?
3. How do you measure whether it went well?

## Recent instance

4. When did this last happen? Walk me through it from the start.
5. What triggered it?
6. What did you do first, and then what?
7. Which tools, people or approvals were involved?
8. Where did it slow down, break or get redone?
9. What did that cost — time, money, risk, a delayed decision?
10. Who noticed, and what did they say?

## Alternatives and history

11. What did you use before this? Why did it change?
12. What have you tried and stopped? What happened?
13. What do you do when the usual approach fails?
14. What would you never change about how this works?

## Buying reality

15. Who pays for tools like this, and from which budget?
16. What does approval require?
17. What has made a purchase easy or impossible before?

## Close

18. Who else experiences this that I should speak with?
19. What should I have asked that I did not?
20. May I follow up with a specific question or artifact?
