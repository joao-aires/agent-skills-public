## Rules

- Multiple pages repeating the same original source count as one origin.
- Record contradicting evidence, not only supporting evidence.
- Distinguish fact from inference.
- Record definition, geography, and time-period mismatches.
- Inaccessible or unverifiable sources should be recorded as failed attempts, not evidence.
- Important derived calculations should cite the input evidence IDs.
