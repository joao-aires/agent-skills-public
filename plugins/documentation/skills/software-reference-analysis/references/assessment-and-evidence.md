# Assess the product through complementary perspectives

Use this reference when defining assessment scope or judging evidence. A dimension is a question asked of a product. It is not a mandatory file, numerical score or assertion of equal research depth.

## Keep the assessment broad enough for its purpose

| Dimension | Explanation to investigate | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy and business | Audience, problem, alternatives, differentiation and the offered value. | Public positioning does not establish demand, retention or internal strategy. |
| Discovery and requirements | Needs, constraints, quality expectations, acceptance and product outcomes. | Distinguish published research from your own hypotheses or proposed study. |
| Website/acquisition | Company versus product entry points, narrative, navigation, proof, pricing and next actions. | Date page observations; a homepage is not the authenticated product. |
| Product UX and information architecture | Objects, roles, navigation scopes, first use, recurring journeys and meaningful states. | Identify exercised tasks, rendered views, source inspection and official previews separately. |
| Interaction, visuals and accessibility | Hierarchy, layout, typography, components, keyboard/focus, feedback and platform adaptation. | Source attributes or screenshots do not establish accessibility conformance or usability. |
| Code/repository architecture | Module responsibilities, dependency contracts, extension mechanisms and stack conventions. | Explain a real change/execution path; directory inventories are supporting context. |
| System architecture | Authority, state, communication, execution, persistence and failure ownership. | Source-configured topology is not observed production deployment. |
| Data/databases | Identity, schemas, queries, consistency, indexing, migration, synchronization and lifecycle. | A declared index is not a measured optimization or complete recovery procedure. |
| AI engineering, where relevant | Context/retrieval, model/tool boundaries, quality assessment, budgets and fallback. | Keep evaluation quality, execution traces and permitted effects distinct. |
| Security/privacy | Identity, enforcement, secrets, sensitive data, trust boundaries, retention and deletion. | Selected enforcement paths are not a full security audit or legal assurance. |
| Reliability/operations | Useful progress, configuration, capacity, overload, diagnosis, recovery and ownership. | A health endpoint or backup job does not establish task recovery. |
| Quality/delivery/own SDLC | Development checks, tests, artifact identity, release/promotion, compatibility and agent-assisted work. | Selling agents is not evidence of using a software factory internally. |
| Customer/company operation | Acquisition, onboarding, support, packaging, pricing, costs and governance. | Do not invent private finances, user research or operating outcomes. |

Read breadth against the actual product. Native clients need lifecycle, permissions and distribution; extensions need execution contexts, grants and host effects; CLIs need commands, process lifetime and terminal feedback. “Not applicable” needs a scope reason. “Not assessed” records absent investigation. “Partial” identifies a consequential open boundary. An inspectable missing principal flow is unfinished analysis, even when the gap is disclosed.

## Classify without collapsing different questions

Record category/audience, task archetypes, application specialization, characteristics, interaction patterns and platforms/technologies separately when they matter. A product can combine several archetypes. Prefer reusable tasks such as authoring, managing records, consuming material, diagnosing execution, completing transactions or assisting work. A tiny resident utility need not inherit the permanent dashboard of a monitoring product.

For example, bookmark capture and browser automation share a platform but have different objects, journeys and effects. A diagnostic console may combine inspection and recovery; its sidebar is a candidate pattern rather than its archetype. Keep missing cohort categories visible instead of assigning a partial reference to establish false coverage.

## Preserve enough provenance to revisit a claim

For code, retain repository, component, branch, full revision and a link to the useful file/symbol near the claim. Resolve mutable references before linking implementation conclusions. Read enough caller/control flow to distinguish a helper from the actual selected path. State feature flags, versions and alternative backends when they change the conclusion.

For web/UI evidence, retain URL, observation date, viewport/platform and relevant state beside the asset or finding. Distinguish official screenshots from your own rendered observation and from an authenticated journey. Preserve unavailable or denied surfaces. A navigation assertion should identify sampled menus/actions rather than imply every route was tested.

| Evidence | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Identity screening | Intended product/source and initial fit. |
| Source walkthrough | Named implementation behavior at the inspected revision. |
| Observed interaction | A sampled action and resulting interface state. |
| Executed fixture | Actual commands, environment, inputs and retained outcome under the stated boundary. |
| Benchmark | A defined workload, baseline, repeated measurements and environment sufficient to interpret them. |

These are different methods, not automatic promotion levels. Preserve failures, skipped checks and unavailable targets. A local state fixture does not prove OS input, browser lifecycle, remote-provider behavior or production durability. Tool success does not establish that a human learned the concept.

Link primary evidence rather than vendoring whole repositories, pages or copyrighted imagery. Check component-code, model-weight, artwork, trademark and hosted-service boundaries separately. Prefer synthetic fixtures; do not retain credentials or private customer payloads. Label your examples and proposed decisions so they cannot be mistaken for upstream observations.

For interaction analysis, [W3C APG patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/) supply widget-level behavior references. They do not classify whole products or certify an observed implementation. Consulted 5 November 2026.
