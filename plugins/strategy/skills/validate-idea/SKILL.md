# Validate an idea

Decide whether a premise is coherent enough to spend more on, and stop at a verdict and an assumption map. The result is not encouragement and not a test result.

Use [business strategy](../business-strategy/SKILL.md) as the logic owner. Apply [idea validation](../business-strategy/references/idea-validation.md), frame the decision with [decision framing](../business-strategy/references/decision-framing.md), and record the work with the [validation plan](../business-strategy/assets/validation-plan.md) when a durable record helps.

When the affected stack, architecture or domain has installed guidance, read it and carry its conclusions. If none exists, use project evidence and primary sources; this activity keeps the outcome, acceptance and stopping point.

## Own the request

Convert the request into a decision: whether to invest further in this premise, for whom, by when, and what "further" would cost. A request to validate a fixed concept is still a request to test the premise, not to confirm it.

## Inputs

- the premise, the customer and the outcome claimed;
- existing evidence — analytics, support and sales records, prior attempts, public sources, founder access — plus the constraint that makes timing matter: runway, market window or a commitment already made.

## Work

1. State the premise in one paragraph: customer, circumstance, required behavior change, mechanism, route to market and deadline. Name its weakest clause.
2. Inventory existing evidence before proposing new work. Classify fact, derived calculation, inference, assumption and unknown. Cite sources and dates.
3. Build a short assumption ledger for claims whose failure would change the decision: importance, confidence, evidence for and against, falsifier, cheapest next test and review date.
4. Check premise coherence: is the customer a reachable group with a budget owner, is the trigger real, is the behavior change plausible, and does the route survive the economics?
5. Recommend **go**, **investigate** or **stop** — or park when the premise may become true later. State the evidence, opportunity cost and what would change the verdict.

Do not interview customers, run a pilot, build a prototype or contact buyers here. If evidence is too thin to judge, the verdict is investigate with a named next step, not an improvised test.

## Output

- premise statement and weakest clause;
- evidence inventory with its limits;
- material assumptions, falsifiers and cheapest tests;
- verdict with reasoning, opportunity cost, review trigger and any state update.

## Stop and hand off

Stop at the premise decision. Hand evidence gaps to [discover-customers](../discover-customers/SKILL.md), market questions to [run-market-scan](../run-market-scan/SKILL.md), and a surviving premise to [test-assumptions](../test-assumptions/SKILL.md). Hand a portfolio-level choice to [set-strategy](../set-strategy/SKILL.md).
