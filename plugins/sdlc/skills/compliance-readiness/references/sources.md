# Governance source guide

These original skills translate applicability and obligations into engineering/control decisions and evidence. They do not reproduce regulatory text, assurance criteria or restricted guidance. Official sources and qualified interpretations control substantive requirements; verify the applicable version and actual facts when a decision depends on them.

## GDPR and regulator guidance

| When to consult | Official source | What it informs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish obligations or interpret a specific provision | [GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) | Material/territorial scope, principles, rights, roles, security and accountability; cite the relevant provision in the assessment |
| Assess geographic applicability | [EDPB territorial scope guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-32018-on-the-territorial-scope-of-the-gdpr-article-3-version-adopted_en) | Article 3 fact patterns; hosting location alone is insufficient |
| Determine actual roles | [EDPB controller/processor guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-072020-on-the-concepts-of-controller-and-processor-in-the-gdpr_en) | Purposes/means and responsibilities for each processing operation |
| Design collection/defaults and the data lifecycle | [EDPB design/default guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-42019-on-article-25-data-protection-by-design-and-by-default_en) | Translating data protection into design and effective defaults |
| Find proportionate operational explanation | [EDPB small-business guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme_en) | Practical entry point; size alone does not establish an exemption |

Follow relevant current guidance from the competent authority for DPIAs, rights, breaches, transfers and sector/national requirements when those concerns arise. Verify authority and applicability; search results or a vendor checklist are not an accepted legal interpretation.

## SOC 2 and assurance

- [AICPA SOC overview](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services): distinguish SOC services and find current official examination resources.
- [Trust Services Criteria](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022): consult the applicable edition for criterion mapping; do not redistribute its text as a skill checklist.
- [Description criteria](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/get-description-criteria-for-your-organizations-soc-2-r-report): establish the required service/system description with the assurance owner/auditor.
- [Illustrative Type 2 report](https://www.aicpa.com/resources/download/illustrative-soc-2-r-report-with-description-and-assertion): understand management assertions, service description, auditor reporting and tests/results; an illustration is not an engagement-specific verdict.

Some official materials require access or licensing. State the precise unavailable reference and the resulting interpretation limit; do not imply that a listing page supplies the full standard. Readiness work can proceed on available evidence while the qualified owner resolves the material gap.
